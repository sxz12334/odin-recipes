Pancakes

Description

These fluffy, lightly sweet pancakes are a simple breakfast favorite. Serve them warm with butter, maple syrup, or fresh fruit.

Mix the batter only until the dry ingredients are moistened; a few small lumps help keep the pancakes tender.

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 3 1/2 teaspoons baking powder
  • 1 tablespoon sugar
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt
  • 1 1/4 cups milk
  • 1 egg
  • 3 tablespoons melted butter, plus more for the pan
  • Maple syrup, for serving

Steps

  1. Whisk together the flour, baking powder, sugar, and salt in a bowl.
  2. In another bowl, whisk the milk, egg, and melted butter.
  3. Pour the wet ingredients into the dry ingredients and stir just until combined.
  4. Warm a lightly buttered skillet over medium heat. Pour about 1/4 cup of batter for each pancake.
  5. Cook until bubbles form and the edges look set, then flip and cook until golden on the other side.
  6. Serve warm with maple syrup.
