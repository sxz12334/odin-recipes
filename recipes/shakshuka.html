Shakshuka

Description

Shakshuka is a one-pan meal of eggs gently cooked in a warmly spiced tomato and pepper sauce. It is hearty enough for brunch or an easy weeknight dinner.

Serve straight from the pan with crusty bread to scoop up the sauce and soft egg yolks.

Ingredients

  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 1 red bell pepper, diced
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 teaspoon ground cumin
  • 1 teaspoon paprika
  • 1 can (28 ounces) crushed tomatoes
  • 4 large eggs
  • Salt and black pepper
  • Chopped parsley, for serving
  • Crusty bread, for serving

Steps

  1. Heat the olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Cook the onion and bell pepper until softened, about 6 to 8 minutes.
  2. Add the garlic, cumin, and paprika. Stir for 30 seconds until fragrant.
  3. Add the tomatoes, season with salt and pepper, and simmer for 10 to 15 minutes until thickened.
  4. Make four small wells in the sauce and crack an egg into each one.
  5. Cover the skillet and cook for 5 to 8 minutes, until the whites are set and the yolks are cooked to your liking.
  6. Sprinkle with parsley and serve with crusty bread.
