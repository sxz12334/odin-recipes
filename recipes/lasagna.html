Lasagna

Description

This comforting lasagna layers pasta, a rich tomato and beef sauce, and creamy cheese. It is baked until bubbling and golden, making a satisfying meal to share.

Prepare the sauce ahead of time to make assembly even easier. Let the lasagna rest briefly after baking so it slices neatly.

Ingredients

  • 9 lasagna noodles
  • 1 pound ground beef
  • 1 small onion, diced
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 can (28 ounces) crushed tomatoes
  • 2 cups ricotta cheese
  • 1 egg
  • 3 cups shredded mozzarella cheese
  • 1/2 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 1 teaspoon dried Italian herbs
  • Salt and black pepper

Steps

  1. Heat the oven to 375°F (190°C). Cook the noodles according to the package directions, then drain.
  2. Brown the beef and onion in a pan over medium heat. Add the garlic and cook for 30 seconds.
  3. Stir in the crushed tomatoes and Italian herbs. Season with salt and pepper, then simmer for 15 minutes.
  4. Mix the ricotta with the egg and half of the Parmesan.
  5. Spread some sauce in a baking dish. Layer noodles, ricotta, sauce, and mozzarella; repeat, finishing with sauce and the remaining cheeses.
  6. Cover with foil and bake for 25 minutes. Uncover and bake for 15 more minutes, then rest for 10 minutes before serving.
